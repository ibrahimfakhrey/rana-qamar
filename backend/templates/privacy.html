Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 15, 2026

1. Introduction

CarePal ("we", "our", or "the app") is a healthcare companion application that connects patients with their caregivers. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our mobile application and related services.

2. Health-Related Features

CarePal is a health-related application that provides the following features:

  • Physical Activity and Fitness: The app allows caregivers to schedule and track physical activity reminders for patients, including exercise routines and daily fitness tasks. Patients receive notifications to complete their activities and can confirm task completion.
  • Weight and Nutrition Tracking: The app collects patient weight and height data as part of their health profile. Caregivers can schedule meal reminders and track patients' dietary habits, including meal times and nutrition-related tasks.
  • Health Condition and Disease Tracking: The app stores information about patients' health conditions and chronic diseases as part of their health profile, enabling caregivers to monitor and manage ongoing health concerns.
  • Healthcare Services and Management: The app serves as a healthcare management platform connecting caregivers with their patients. Caregivers can create and manage comprehensive health schedules, monitor task completion, and coordinate care activities for one or more patients.
  • Medication and Treatment Management: The app enables caregivers to set up medication schedules for patients, including medication names, dosages, and reminder times. Patients receive timely medication reminders and can confirm when they have taken their medications.

3. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, and login credentials for both caregivers and patients.
  • Health and Fitness Data: Patient health profiles including age, gender, weight, height, health conditions, and chronic diseases.
  • Nutrition and Meal Data: Meal schedules, meal types, and dietary reminders configured by caregivers.
  • Medication Data: Medication names, dosages, schedules, and confirmation records.
  • Activity and Exercise Data: Physical activity schedules, exercise types, durations, and completion records.
  • Hydration Data: Water intake reminders and tracking.
  • Usage Data: Task confirmations, notification interactions, and app usage patterns.
  • Device Information: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens for delivering push notifications.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain the CarePal healthcare management service
  • Send medication reminders and track medication adherence
  • Send meal and nutrition reminders to patients
  • Send physical activity and exercise reminders to patients
  • Send hydration (water intake) reminders to patients
  • Enable caregivers to monitor and manage patient health schedules
  • Track and display patient health conditions and chronic diseases
  • Notify caregivers when patients confirm their health tasks
  • Authenticate users and secure their accounts

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secure servers. We use industry-standard security measures including:

  • Encrypted password storage using secure hashing algorithms
  • JWT (JSON Web Token) authentication for API access
  • HTTPS encryption for all data transmission

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal or health information with third parties. Your data is only accessible to:

  • The patient (their own data)
  • The assigned caregiver (their patients' data)

7. Push Notifications

CarePal uses Firebase Cloud Messaging to send health reminders and task notifications. You can disable push notifications at any time through your device settings.

8. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you wish to delete your account and associated data, please contact your caregiver or reach out to us directly.

9. Children's Privacy

CarePal may be used by caregivers to manage health schedules for minors under their care. We do not knowingly collect data from children without caregiver consent.

10. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Opt out of push notifications

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
